About Greenfield Clinic

A fictional medical-clinic portal built as a safe, deliberately-vulnerable target for the FixFort demonstration.

It ships with three planted weaknesses — SQL injection on staff login, stored XSS in patient comments, and a PHP2Shell RCE on document upload (CVE-2026-109618). FixFort scans for them, proves a temporary shield rule in its Practice Room, and — once a human approves — blocks the attack in minutes while real staff keep working.

🔒No real people or data. Runs on loopback only. Never expose this app to a network.
